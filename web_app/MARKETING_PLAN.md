### Strategy 2: Direct School Visits (35% of In-Person Budget)
**Timeline**: Ongoing throughout the year

#### Outreach Process

**Phase 1: Prospecting (Weeks 1-2)**
- Identify 150 schools in target market
- Research decision-maker names (LinkedIn, school websites)
- Compile contact list with phone/email

**Phase 2: Initial Contact (Week 3-4)**
- Send personalized email to principal/director
- Subject: "Free Technology Assessment for [School Name]"
- Follow up with phone call 3 days later
- Goal: 40-50 meetings (33% conversion)

**Phase 3: School Visits (Weeks 5-12)**
- Schedule 40-50 in-person meetings
- Duration: 20-30 minutes
- Attendees: Principal + IT Manager (ideal)
- Bring: Laptop demo, printed materials, free trial offer

**Phase 4: Follow-up (Ongoing)**
- Send thank you email within 24 hours
- Provide free trial (2 weeks)
- Weekly check-ins during trial
- Customized proposal if interested

#### Sales Kit for School Visits
1. **One-page product overview**
2. **Cost savings calculator** (customized for school size)
3. **Customer testimonial cards** (3-5 local schools)
4. **ROI calculator** (time saved, efficiency gains)
5. **Free trial card** (QR code to sign up)
6. **Comparison chart** vs. manual systems
7. **Implementation timeline** (quick deployment)

#### Target Selection Criteria
- ✅ School size: 100+ students
- ✅ Currently using manual systems or outdated software
- ✅ Growing/expanding school
- ✅ School with active parent involvement
- ✅ Recently appointed new principal/director

## 📞 Cold Calling & Outreach Strategy

### Outreach Process
1. **Research** (30 min per school)
   - Identify principal/director name
   - Find phone number + email
   - Note school size & current system

2. **First Call** (Script below)
   - Introduce self & company
   - Ask qualifying questions
   - Offer free assessment
   - Schedule meeting if interested

3. **Email Follow-up**
   - Send within 1 hour of call
   - Personalized subject line
   - Clear next steps

4. **Second Call** (If no response)
   - Call 3 days after email
   - More direct offer
   - Shorter call duration

5. **Meeting Preparation**
   - Customize presentation for school type
   - Prepare demo of relevant features
   - Bring sales materials

### Cold Calling Script
```
"Hi [Principal Name], I'm [Your Name] with Gaybeck Starkids. 
I'm reaching out to [School Name] because we work with schools like yours 
to reduce administrative work and improve operations. 

Do you currently use any software for student/staff management? 
[Listen to response]

The reason I'm calling is we help schools save up to 10 hours per week 
on administrative tasks. Would it make sense to have a quick 20-minute 
conversation to see if we're a fit? 

How does [Day] at [Time] look for you?"
```

### Call Targets & Timeline
- **Week 1-4**: Call 150 schools (40 calls/week)
- **Target Conversion**: 20-30% agreement to meeting (30-45 meetings)
- **Meeting Conversion**: 30-40% → paid trial
- **Trial Conversion**: 50-60% → paying customers
- **Final Result**: 8-15 new customers in first month
